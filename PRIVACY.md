# Privacy — Atlas for Rhythia

Version of November 23, 2026. The app shows this text under Terms; after changing it run `npm run terms`.

Atlas has no ads, no tracking and no accounts of its own. Nothing about you is sold or shared. Questions: [contact@shuriel.com](mailto:contact@shuriel.com).

## On your computer

Your library, collections, copies and settings stay on your computer. If you sign in to Rhythia, the sign-in is kept in the Windows password store.

## What goes out

- **Rhythia:** maps, collections and your sign-in, as on the website.
- **Shuriel's server:** once a day, a random key and the version of the map types list. For Played near your rank, Near your rank and Your types, your public Rhythia player number; it answers with totals of players ranked near you, never their names. For At which speed and For you, the same number and the Rhythia number, stars and notes of the maps on screen; it answers from your public Rhythia top scores. It sees your IP address to keep its limits and block misuse; its logs keep only part of it.
- **GitHub:** once an hour, whether a new version is out.

Reimagined and Atlas talk only on your computer.

## Removing it

In Settings → Data, press Uninstall Atlas and leave **Also delete my Atlas data** ticked: Atlas, its data and your Rhythia sign-in are removed. From Windows Settings → Apps, tick **Delete the application data** for the same.
