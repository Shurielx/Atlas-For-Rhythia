# Terms of use — Atlas for Rhythia

Version of November 22, 2026. The app shows this text under Terms; after changing it run `npm run terms`.

Atlas is a free app by Shuriel for managing your Rhythia maps, scores and collections. It is not made by the Rhythia team. Contact: [contact@shuriel.com](mailto:contact@shuriel.com).

## Using Atlas

Your account follows Rhythia's rules. Atlas never touches the game program or your login file. It changes the game's files only when you ask, with the game closed.

What you download, delete or change with Atlas is up to you. Keep your own backups.

## Limits

Atlas downloads at most 3000 maps a day and sends at most 10 requests a second to Rhythia. Song previews of maps you don't have are limited to 300 a day.

Atlas is open source (GPL-3.0). If you run a changed copy, for example one without these limits, it is at your own risk and you get no help; Rhythia or Shuriel's server can block your account or address. Shuriel's server and the Atlas name and logo are only for the official Atlas.

## Signing in

Signing in is optional. Atlas never asks for or keeps your password.
